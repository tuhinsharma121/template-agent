2. HEALTH TIPS
--------------
Based on your BMI category, here are some actionable and encouraging tips to support your health journey:

- **Focus on sustainable changes:** Aim for a gradual, healthy weight loss of about 0.5–1 kg per week.
- **Mindful eating:** Try gently reducing portion sizes and keeping an eye on your daily intake to build awareness.
- **Stay active:** Gradually increase your physical activity, working towards 300 minutes of moderate exercise per week (like brisk walking, swimming, or cycling).
- **Nourish your body:** Swap out refined carbs for nutrient-rich whole grains, fresh vegetables, and lean proteins.
- **Limit processed foods:** Try to reduce added sugars and highly processed foods when possible.
- **Build lasting habits:** Focus on building long-term, healthy routines rather than looking for quick fixes.
- **What to avoid:** Steer clear of crash diets, extreme restrictions, or exercise routines that feel unsustainable. Be kind to yourself!
